# Mise En Abyme (MEA)

## Welcome To Mise En Abyme!

### YOUR Virtual Cloud Desktop

Mise En Abyme was presented as a virtual cloud desktop concept blending software, hardware, and the web. This page preserves the wording and graphics from the archived Heraldic MEA website. The statements are historical and are not claims that the product or services are currently available.

#### Original feature statements

- Blends Software, Hardware and the Web
- Streams YOUR games from the cloud
- Turns your smartphone into a desktop computer
- Features next-gen security
- Engineered by Cloud+ certified team
- Uses IPv6 Internet addresses
